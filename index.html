About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Sociology at Virginia Tech, where I study economic sociology, digital sociology, and the sociology of work, labor, and organizations. My research asks how economic value is produced, measured, and unequally distributed — across labor markets, platform and influencer economies, and the moral evaluation of care work.
